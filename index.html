📖 Step-by-Step Checklist

Step 1: Buying at the Butcher • Ask for fresh bone-in cuts (5–10 days from slaughter), NOT old vacuum bags sitting in blood liquid.
• Ask the butcher to leave the bones ON and the full fat cap UNTRIMMED.
• Pick female cattle (Heifer) or good marbling (MB 3+).
Step 2: Cleaning the MA400 Fridge • Take the pink Himalayan salt block out before cleaning.
• Wipe inside walls, rubber door seals & wire racks with warm water, then spray with 70% Food-Grade Ethanol (or no-rinse brewing sanitiser).
• Never use bleach or scented kitchen sprays. Run fridge empty for 12 hours first.
Step 3: Prepping the Meat (Day 0) • Put on clean disposable gloves.
• NEVER wash meat with tap water. Pat completely bone-dry with clean paper towels.
• Cut off any thin dangling meat flaps.
• Place on wire shelf: Fat Cap UP, Bones DOWN with an 8cm gap around all sides.
