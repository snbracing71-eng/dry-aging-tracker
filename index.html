🩺 "Is This Safe?" Mold & Odor Check

⚪ White Chalky Specs / Patchy White Dust ✅ SAFE: Either harmless salt crystals or beneficial white Penicillium mold. Leave it or wipe gently with vinegar/ethanol cloth at harvest.
🟢 Green, Black, or Tall Fuzzy Mold 🚫 DANGER: Spoilage mold. Remove piece immediately; if localized on outer fat, excuse 2cm deep around spot, sanitize chamber with 70% ethanol, and check fan airflow.
💧 Sticky, Slimy, or Wet Surface ⚠️ HUMIDITY TOO HIGH (>85% RH): Pat dry with paper towel, ensure UVC is ON, verify 8cm air gap around meat, and lower RH setpoint to 75–80%.
🪨 Thick Leathery Outer Ring (Case Hardening) ⚠️ HUMIDITY TOO LOW (<68% RH): Outside dried too fast, trapping moisture inside. Raise RH to 80–82%.
👃 Nutty / Blue Cheese vs. Sour / Putrid Smell ✅ Nutty, earthy, roasted hazelnut, mild blue cheese: Normal enzymatic dry-aging aroma.
🚫 Sharp sour, ammonia, or rotting odor: Spoilage near the bone. Discard affected section.

📖 Master Butcher Guide & Backup

💾 Data Backup & Restore (Phone Memory) Save a backup file of all your fridges, active batches, and freezer logs, or restore from a file:
1. Selecting Meat & Day 0 Prep • Buy fresh bone-in cuts (5–10 days post-slaughter) with full fat cap intact.
• Never wash with tap water. Blot bone-dry with paper towels.
• Load on wire racks Fat Side UP, Bones DOWN with 8cm spacing.
2. Post-Harvest Storage Rules • 🍽️ Unsealed in Fridge (Ready to Eat): Wrap in butcher paper or on a rack; cook within 8–10 days max.
• ❄️ Vacuum Sealed (Cryo Chilled): Keeps in regular fridge (0–3°C) for 30–45 days.
• 🧊 Deep Frozen (-18°C): Vacuum-sealed steaks last 12+ months without freezer burn.
