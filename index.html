🩺 Is This Safe? Troubleshooter

⚪ White chalky specs / light white dust:
✅ SAFE. Beneficial noble mold or salt crystals. Wipe gently with weak brine or leave until trim.
🟢 Green, black, or tall fuzzy mold:
🚫 DANGER. Spoilage mold. Remove immediately; if superficial, wipe with 75% food-grade alcohol and trim deeply; if pervasive, discard.
💧 Sticky, slimy, or clammy surface:
⚠️ HUMIDITY TOO HIGH (>82% RH) or poor airflow. Blot dry with paper towel, ensure 8cm spacing, and check fan/RH.
🪨 Thick rock-hard outer ring, wet raw center:
⚠️ CASE HARDENING (RH <70%). Raise humidity to 78–80% RH to let internal moisture equalize.
👃 Nutty / buttery / blue-cheese vs. Sour / putrid:
✅ Roasted hazelnut, buttered popcorn, or earthy funk is normal. 🚫 Sharp sour, ammonia, or rotting odor means spoilage.

🔧 Chamber Maintenance

📋 Master Butcher's Field Guide

1. Cabinet Prep & Hygiene: Sanitize shelves & walls with 70% food-grade ethanol before loading. Keep UVC ON for Meat & Fish, OFF for Salami & Cheese.
2. Buying Rules: Buy fresh bone-in sub-primals (5–10d post-slaughter) with intact fat caps. Avoid old cryovac meat sitting in blood purge.
3. Loading Orientation: Blot meat bone-dry with paper towels (never rinse with water). Place Fat Side UP, Bone DOWN with 8cm airflow clearance.
4. Post-Harvest Lifecycle: Once harvested, weighing and photo buttons lock automatically. Track Ready-to-Eat Fridge (8–10d), Vacuum Chilled (45d), or Deep Frozen (12m), then file to your Archive Vault when consumed!
