🔧 Chamber Maintenance
• 2-Hour Pre-Cleaning Cycle: Run a 2-hour defrost & dry pre-cleaning cycle after each full run before wiping down with cleaner (dries the interior completely and reduces power consumption!).
• Pink Salt Block: Position under hanging meat to stabilize humidity; wash & dry between runs.
• Carbon Filter & UVC Bulb: Replace carbon filter every 6 months and UVC bulb every 12 months.
🩺 Spore Alert & Safety Check
🚨 SPORE ALERT — ZERO MOLD ON RAW MEAT:
• While noble mold belongs on specific salami or cheese, mold has NO business on raw dry-aged meat! Over 250 fungal species produce 300+ poisonous mycotoxins.
• NEVER JUST TRIM MOLD OFF RAW MEAT: Visible white or black furry mold is only the tip of the iceberg—by then, the mycelia (fungal roots) have already permeated deep into the meat tissue. If white or black furry mold appears on raw meat, the entire cut must be disposed of!

✅ Good Signs on Meat: Deep mahogany dry crust, nutty/buttery/earthy aroma, firm dry pellicle.

🧀 Cheese Rule: Cheese varieties that normally age without mold (Gouda, Cheddar, Pecorino, Manchego) must be discarded if blue or black mold appears. Brush normal surface rind weekly with a soft bristle brush inside a plastic tub.
📋 Master Guide & Data Backup
⏱️ When to Load Post-Slaughter:
• Load right after rigor mortis subsides (6–8h pork, 36–40h beef): 1–2 days after slaughter for Pork, Lamb & Poultry; 2–3 days after slaughter for Beef.

🐂 Best Cattle & Butcher's 10 Questions:
• Heifer (18–22m, 400–500kg): Very Good (Top Pick).
• Ox (24m+, up to 1,200kg) & 10+ Year Old Cattle: Good / Very Good.
• Ask for Tenderstretch / Pelvic suspension (stretches steak muscles during rigor mortis) and stress-free slaughter (up to 75% of quality; stress depletes glycogen/ATP and causes dark, firm, dry DFD meat that cannot dry-age).

🧪 Dry Aging (pH 6.0) vs. Wet Aging (pH 5.5):
• Dry aging raises pH to 6.0 (nutty, meaty, retains juices when sliced, unlocks raw umami/MSG/IMP & kokumi even in carpaccio/tartare; 8–12% bone-in weight loss).
• Wet aging in a bag drops pH to 5.5 (sour, metallic, bloody; turns mushy after 3 weeks).
• Tenderness Peak (80%): 21d beef, 12–16d game, 10d veal, 3d poultry. Flavor shoots straight up from Week 3 to Week 5 (Intense) and plateaus at Week 8 (Intense+).

🪝 Stocking & Drilling Spine Bone:
• Wear fresh single-use gloves; wipe away all bone sawdust.
• Use a disinfected drill to bore through the vertebral body (spine bone) and hang with a swivel hook so the meat is never punctured! Whole backs weigh 25kg+.
• Co-Aging Rules: Fresh beef, pork, lamb & poultry can share a basic run. Hams and raw sausages can share ONLY if no sausages have noble mold.
