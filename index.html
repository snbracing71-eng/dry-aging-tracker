📖 Dry Aging Bible Checklist

1. Buying at the Butcher (What to Ask) • Ask for fresh sub-primals (5–10 days post-slaughter), NOT old cryovac bags sitting in blood purge for weeks.
• Keep bones ON and full dorsal fat cap UNTRIMMED.
• Trim off any thin dangling meat flaps so they don't trap moisture.
2. Sanitising the MA400 Cabinet • Remove Himalayan salt block before spraying.
• Wipe walls, rubber seals & wire racks with warm water, then spray with 70% Food-Grade Ethanol or no-rinse food sanitiser.
• Never use bleach or scented sprays. Pre-run fridge empty for 12h.
3. Prepping the Cut (Day 0) • Wear clean nitrile gloves.
• NEVER rinse with tap water. Dab completely bone-dry with paper towels.
• Place on rack: Fat Cap UP, Bones DOWN with 8cm air gap around all sides.
