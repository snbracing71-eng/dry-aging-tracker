📖 Master Butcher's Guide & Backup

1. Buying & Selection Rules • Skip Old Cryovac Meat: Buy cuts freshly broken down (5–10 days post-slaughter). Meat wet-aged in blood purge for 3+ weeks develops sour/metallic lactic notes.
• Bone-In & Fat Cap Intact: Bones and thick dorsal fat shield the eye muscle so you only trim outer crust, not expensive steak.
2. Cabinet Hygiene & Loading • Sanitise First: Wipe racks, hooks, and walls with food-safe sanitiser or 70% alcohol before loading.
• Airflow & Hook Rules: Leave 5–8cm spacing between pieces. Wipe away all bone sawdust after sawing. Always pierce hooks through the bone—never pierce the meat flesh.
• UVC Light Rule: Keep UVC ON for Meat, Poultry & Fish. Keep UVC STRICTLY OFF for Salami & Cheese.
3. How Basic / Intense / Intense+ Climates Work • Basic (1.5°C | 80–82% RH): Higher humidity keeps outer pellicle thin for maximum yield & gentle tenderisation.
• Intense (1.8°C | 75–78% RH): Drier air draws out more free water to concentrate roasted-hazelnut & buttery umami flavour.
• Intense+ (2.5°C–3.0°C | 72–75% RH): Slightly warmer temp & lower RH accelerates natural enzymes (calpains) to achieve deep steakhouse funk in the exact same number of days!
4. Harvest, Trimming & Storage Lifespans • Pellicle Excision: Use a stiff boning knife to shave off all dark, leathery outer crust (1–3mm).
• In Fridge — Ready to Eat (Unsealed): Trimmed steaks or roasts kept in the fridge on a rack/plate last 8–10 days max. Cook before Day 10!
• Vacuum Sealed & Chilled: Lasts 30–45 days at 0–2°C.
• Deep Frozen (-18°C): Lasts 12+ months in vacuum bags with zero cell-wall damage.
