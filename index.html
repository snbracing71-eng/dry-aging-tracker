🩺 Spore Alert & Safety Checker

🚨 Spore Alert Rule on Raw Meat: Over 250 fungal species produce 300+ poisonous mycotoxins. If white, green, or black furry mold appears on raw meat, the mycelia (fungal roots) have already spread deep into the tissue—do NOT just trim the crust; the entire cut must be disposed of and the fridge sanitized!
✅ SAFE ON RAW MEAT: Dry mahogany-dark leathery pellicle crust, white crystalline salt/tyrosine flecks, roasted hazelnut/buttery aroma.

✅ NOBLE MOLD (Salami/Cheese Only): Chalky white Penicillium curing culture belongs ONLY on specific salami or cheese runs with UVC OFF.

🚫 UNSAFE (Discard Immediately): Furry white, green, or black mold spots on raw meat; sour/ammonia/putrid odor; sticky slimy wet surface.

🔧 Fridge Maintenance ()

💡 2-Hour Pre-Cleaning Rule: Run a 2-hour defrost & dry pre-cleaning cycle before wiping down between batches (saves power & dries interior). Never use bleach or citrus sprays; use 70% food-grade ethanol/sanitiser.

📋 Master Butcher & Aging Guide

1. Post-Slaughter Timing & Buying Rules:
• Load Pork, Lamb & Poultry 1–2 days post-slaughter; load Beef 2–3 days post-slaughter (after rigor mortis subsides).
• Choose Heifer (18–22m) or Ox (24m+) with good marbling. Avoid stressed DFD (Dark, Firm, Dry) meat. Dry aging raises pH to 6.0 (nutty, juicy, raw-edible umami) vs wet-aged pH 5.5 (sour/metallic).

2. Prep, Stocking & Hanging:
• Wear nitrile gloves. Never wash meat with tap water. Dab bone-dry and trim loose flaps.
• Shelf: Fat cap UP, Bones DOWN. Hanging: Drill through the vertebral spine bone with a disinfected bit and hang with a swivel S-hook (never puncture meat).

3. Post-Harvest Storage Lifespans:
• Presentation in Ager (Unpackaged): 5–7 days max on shelf.
• Ready-to-Eat Regular Fridge: 8–10 days.
• Vacuum-Sealed in Ager: 14 days (or 30–45d chilled at 0–2°C). Fold bag cuff over before inserting meat & cover sharp bones with a canvas guard.
• Deep Frozen (-18°C Vacuum-Sealed): Up to 3 years with zero juice loss.
