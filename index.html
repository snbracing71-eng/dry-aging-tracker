🩺 Safety & Spore Alert Guide

🚨 THE "SPORE ALERT" MOLD RULE • ANY White, Green, or Black Furry Mold on Raw Beef/Pork/Lamb = DISPOSE OF THE WHOLE CUT IMMEDIATELY!
• Unlike salami, mold on raw meat sends invisible toxic root threads (mycelia) deep into the muscle that trimming cannot remove.
• Clean and sanitize the entire fridge with 70% ethanol before starting a new batch.
✅ Normal & Safe Signs • Hard, dry, dark mahogany or black-red outer crust (pellicle).
• Smells like roasted hazelnuts, buttered popcorn, mild blue cheese, or rich prosciutto.
• White crystalline salt/tyrosine dots inside aged beef or cheese (100% safe flavor crystals).
🚫 Bad Smells vs. Cryovac Sourness • Putrid / Rotten Egg / Ammonia smell: Spoiled—discard.
• Sour / Metallic smell right out of a vacuum bag: Normal lactic acid buildup (pH ~5.5). Run the 3-Day Wet-Aged Regeneration cycle to restore a clean beef aroma!

🔧 Fridge Maintenance

🧼 2-Hour Pre-Cleaning Cycle (12°C • 85% RH) Run the empty fridge at 12°C and 85% RH for 2 hours to soften dried fat/proteins before wiping down with 70% food-grade ethanol. Remove pink salt blocks before cleaning!
💡 UVC Sterilization Bulb & Salt Check • Replace UVC bulb every 12 months.
• Empty any liquid pooling in the stainless salt tray so humidity stays rock solid.

📋 Master Dry Aging Guide

🔒 Strict 1-Category Rule Per Fridge • Never mix categories in the same fridge. Meat (1.5°C), Seafood (0.5°C), Salami (12°C, UVC OFF), and Cheese (11°C, UVC OFF) require completely different climates and hygiene rules.
🥩 Simple Fat / Marbling Guide (Why It Matters) • Lean (MB 0–1): Little/no white fat flecks inside the red meat. Ages fast (7–28d). Do NOT use Intense+ or it will dry into jerky.
• Angus (MB 2–4): Moderate white fat flecks throughout. Ideal for 28–42 day classic steakhouse aging.
• Wagyu (MB 5+): Heavy white fat webbing. Protects the muscle for ultra-long 42–56 day aging.
🐄 Best Cattle & Slaughter Freshness Rule • Heifer (Young Female) & Ox: Top choice for tender, heavily marbled beef.
• Slaughter Window: Put Pork/Lamb/Poultry in the fridge 1–2 days post-slaughter, and Beef 2–5 days post-slaughter (or wet-aged <10d bone-in / <21d deboned).
🧪 pH Value & 3-Day Wet-Aged Regeneration • Dry aging keeps meat around pH 6.0 (buttery, nutty). Wet-aging in cryovac drops pH to 5.5 (sour/metallic).
• Putting wet-aged meat (>3 weeks in bag) into the dry aging fridge for 3 Days evaporates the sour bag juice and forms a crisp crust!
