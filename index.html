📖 MA400 Master Checklist

1. Active Dry Aging (MA400) • Temp: 1.5°C–2°C | RH: 75%–80% | UVC ON.
• Place Fat Cap UP, Bones DOWN with 8cm air clearance. Never wash meat with water.
2. Harvesting & Pellicle Excision • Weigh final cut, trim 1–2mm dark outer pellicle crust, and slice thick steaks (38–50mm).
3. Storage & Freezer Traceability • Vacuum-seal steaks: keeps chilled 30–45 days or frozen at -18°C for 12+ months with zero purge loss.
