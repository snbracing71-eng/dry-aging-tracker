🩺 Safety Check
✅ Good Signs: Deep mahogany crust, nutty/buttery/earthy aroma, firm dry pellicle.

⚠️ Warning Signs: Green/black fuzzy mould, slimy/sticky surface, sour or ammonia smell (check 1.5°C setpoint & airflow immediately).
📋 Guide & Backup
Chamber Rules: Never mix Red Meat, Fish, Salami, or Cheese in the same chamber at the same time. Keep 8cm air gaps between cuts.
