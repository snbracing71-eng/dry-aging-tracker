🩺 "Is This Safe?" Mold & Spoilage Guide

⚪ White Chalky / Powdery Coat: Beneficial Thamnidium / Penicillium mold. Enhances tenderness & nutty aroma. Safe—wipe with brine or trim pellicle before cooking.
🟢🔴 Green, Black or Fuzzy Tall Mold: Undesirable surface mold. Remove batch immediately, trim 1.5cm below affected spot, and sanitize chamber with UVC + vinegar/alcohol wipe.
💧 Slimy / Sticky Surface: Humidity too high or airflow blocked. Pat dry, verify fan circulation, and check RH is not exceeding target.
🧱 Thick Hard Outer Ring (Case Hardening): RH too low or fan blowing directly on lean muscle. Raise RH by 3–5%.
👃 Smell Check: Earthy, nutty, buttery, blue-cheese = GOOD. Sour, ammonia, putrid = SPOILED (discard immediately).

📋 Master Butcher's Field Guide & Backup

🥩 Beef & Game: 1.5°C • 78–82% RH • Always age on the bone with fat cap intact where possible for 35–56 days.
🐖 Pork & 🐑 Lamb: 1.5°C • 78–80% RH • Shorter 14–28 day cycles protect delicate fat from oxidation.
🐟 Seafood: 1.0°C • 75% RH • Scale, gut, scrape bloodline from spine, hang 5–14 days.
🥓 Salami & Charcuterie: 12.0°C • 75% RH • Ferment first, then hang for slow moisture loss (30–38% total loss).
🧀 Cheese Cave: 11.0°C • 85% RH • Turn wheels every 3–5 days.
