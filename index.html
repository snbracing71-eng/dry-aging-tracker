🩺 Spore Alert & Safety Checker

🚨 Golden Rule of Dry Aging: Any batch showing unsafe green, black, or slimy/fuzzy mold cannot be rescued by wiping—it must be discarded immediately, and the fridge must be emptied and sanitized before reuse.

✅ SAFE (Normal): Chalky white noble mold (Thamnidium), deep mahogany/dark crust, nutty/buttery/earthy aroma.

🚫 UNSAFE (Discard Immediately): Green, black, or tall fuzzy mold spots; sour/putrid/ammonia smell; sticky slimy wet surface.
