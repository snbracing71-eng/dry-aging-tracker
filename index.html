🩺 "Is This Safe?" Spore Alert & Guide

🚨 SPORE ALERT — White, Black or Green Furry Mold on Raw Meat: Mold has NO business on raw dry-aged meat (over 250 fungal species produce 300+ mycotoxins). Visible mold is only the tip of the iceberg—the mycelia (fungal roots) have already spread deep into the meat tissue. Do NOT just trim the crust—dispose of the entire cut immediately and sterilize the chamber.
🧂 White Crystalline Salt / Tyrosine Flecks: Hard, flat, non-fuzzy crystal specs on dry fat or lean muscle are harmless salt/amino-acid crystals—not mold.
🥓 Noble White Mold (Salami / Specific Hams Only): Powdery white Penicillium nalgiovense belongs ONLY on inoculated salami/charcuterie runs—never co-age noble-mold sausages with raw meat.
💧 Slimy / Sticky Surface: Humidity too high (>85% RH) or airflow blocked. Pat dry, ensure pieces aren't touching, and verify fan circulation.
🧱 Thick Hard Outer Ring (Case Hardening): Humidity too low (<70% RH). Raise RH by 3–5% so interior moisture can escape evenly.
👃 Smell Check: Roasted hazelnut, browned butter, earthy, mild blue-cheese = GOOD. Sour, ammonia, putrid = SPOILED.

🔧 Chamber Maintenance & Pre-Clean

🧼 2-Hour Pre-Cleaning Cycle (P16): Before wiping down your cabinet between batches, run a 2-hour defrost & dry pre-cleaning cycle. This dries the interior completely and reduces power consumption afterward.

📋 Master Butcher's Field Guide & Backup

⏱️ Best Time to Load Post-Slaughter: Load Pork, Lamb & Poultry 1–2 days after slaughter, and Beef 2–3 days after slaughter (right after rigor mortis subsides).
🧬 Dry (pH 6.0) vs. Wet Aging (pH 5.5): Dry-aged beef rises to pH 6.0 (nutty, buttery, enhanced Maillard crust, zero juice loss). Wet-aged bag meat stays acidic at pH 5.5 (sour/metallic). Run a 3-Day Wet-Aged Regeneration cycle in the ager to remove bag sourness!
🪝 Drilling & Hanging Tip: Use a disinfected drill to bore through the vertebral spine bone and hang with a swivel S-hook so zero meat is punctured.
🐄 10 Butcher Questions ("Do The Grilling"): Ask for Heifer (18–22m) or Ox (24m+), stress-free slaughter (prevents DFD high-pH meat), Tenderstretch/pelvic suspension, and grass-fed with grain finish.
