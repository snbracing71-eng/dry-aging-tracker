🩺 "Is This Safe?" Troubleshooter

⚪ White Chalky Specs / Fine White Dust:
✅ SAFE. Beneficial Thamnidium mold or harmless salt crystallization. Releases proteases that tenderize meat and create nutty flavor. Trim away at harvest.
🟢 Green, Black, or Tall Fuzzy Spots:
🚨 DANGER. Spoilage contamination. Remove cut immediately; if superficial, wipe with vinegar/saline and trim generously, or discard if deep and putrid. Sanitize cabinet & verify UVC is ON.
💧 Slimy or Sticky Surface:
⚠️ HUMIDITY TOO HIGH (>82% RH) or Poor Airflow. Ensure 8cm gap between cuts, verify fan is unobstructed, and drop RH to 75–78%.
🪨 Hard Thick Outer Ring, Wet Raw Center:
⚠️ CASE HARDENING (Humidity <70% RH). Exterior dried too fast and locked moisture inside. Raise RH to 78–80%.
👃 Aroma Check:
✅ Good: Nutty, buttery popcorn, earthy prosciutto, mild blue cheese.
🚨 Bad: Sour milk, ammonia, sharp rotting garbage.

📋 Master Butcher's Guide & Backup

1. Cabinet Hygiene & Loading: Sanitize shelves and walls between runs. Leave 8cm air gaps between cuts. Place fat-cap UP and bones DOWN on stainless racks.
2. Selecting Sub-Primals: Always buy bone-in, untrimmed primal cuts with thick external fat caps and high intramuscular marbling. Never dry-age thin individual steaks (except thick Chuck/Neck steaks over 4–7 days).
3. Post-Harvest Storage Limits:
• Unsealed in Fridge (Ready-to-Eat): Max 8–10 days.
• Vacuum-Sealed Chilled: 30–45 days at 1–2°C.
• Vacuum-Sealed Deep Frozen (-18°C): 12+ months with zero drip loss.
