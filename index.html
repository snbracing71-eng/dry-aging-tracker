🩺 Safety Check
✅ Good Signs: Deep mahogany crust, nutty/buttery/earthy aroma, firm dry pellicle.

⚠️ Warning Signs: Green/black fuzzy mould, slimy/sticky surface, sour or ammonia smell (check 1.5°C setpoint & airflow immediately).

🧀 Cheese Rule of Thumb: Cheese varieties that normally age without mold (Gouda, Cheddar, Pecorino, Manchego) should be discarded if they suddenly display unexpected blue or black mold. Brush off normal surface rind dust weekly with a soft bristle brush inside a plastic tub.
📋 Dry Aging Bible Guide
🐂 Best Cattle for Dry Aging (Book Table):
• Heifer (Female, 18–22m, 400–500kg): Very Good (Top Pick).
• Ox (Castrated Male, 24m+, up to 1,200kg): Good / Very Good.
• Yearling / Young Bull / Cow: Average.
• Suckling Calf / Calf: Less Good.

🥩 Marbling Grades (BMS):
• MG1 (BMS 2): Light marbling • MG2 (BMS 4): Medium marbling (Standard book chart target) • MG3 (BMS 6+): Heavy marbling.

💧 Wet-Aged Vacuum Meat Tip:
Meat aged in a vacuum bag under 3 weeks can be matured in the Dry Ager to minimize sour-metallic low-pH notes and restore a clean, natural taste.

🚫 Never Dry Age Individual Steaks:
Always age whole bone-in or fat-covered sub-primals first, then slice into Porterhouse, Ribeye, Denver, Chuck Eye, or Santa Fe steaks at harvest!
