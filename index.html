📖 Dry Aging & Storage Reference

1. Chamber Prep & Placement
• Sanitise interior with 70% food-grade ethanol.
• Never rinse meat with tap water; pat bone-dry with paper towel.
• Place Fat Cap UP, Bones DOWN on racks with 8cm airflow gap.
2. Strictly One Category Per Fridge
• Do not mix Fish, Salami, or Cheese with Red Meat.
• Salami & Cheese require UVC Light OFF to protect beneficial surface cultures.
3. Trimming, Vacuum Sealing & Freezing
• Trim 1–2mm dark outer pellicle before slicing into 38–50mm steaks.
• Vacuum-sealed dry-aged steaks hold 30–45 days refrigerated or 12+ months frozen at -18°C with zero purge loss.
