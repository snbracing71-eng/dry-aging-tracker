🥩 AgerPro Says

📖 AgerPro Checklist

1. Prep: UVC ON for Meat/Poultry/Fish (OFF for Salami & Cheese). Wipe bone dust clean; hook through bone, never meat.

2. Climates:
• Basic (1.5°C | 80–82% RH): Gentle maturation & maximum yield.
• Intense (1.8°C | 75–78% RH): Concentrates nutty steakhouse flavor.
• Intense+ (2.5°C–3.0°C | 72–75% RH): Warmer temp & lower RH speeds up enzymes for deeper funk in the same days.
• Salami (12°C–14°C | 75–82% RH | UVC OFF): Harvest by target weight loss % (25%–40% loss).
• Cheese (10°C–12°C | 85–90% RH | UVC OFF): Universal cave setting; time is open-ended—just flip wheels every 3–4 days.
