🩺 Spore Alert & Safety Check

🚨 SPORE ALERT — RAW MEAT MOLD RULE:
Over 250 fungal species produce 300+ poisonous mycotoxins. On raw aging meat, zero furry white, green, or black mold is allowed. Once visible on raw meat, invisible fungal roots (mycelia) have already penetrated deep into the muscle tissue—trimming the surface is NOT enough and the entire cut must be disposed of. (Beneficial noble white mold only belongs on inoculated salami or cheese in a separate run).
✅ Safe Signs: Hard dry mahogany crust (pellicle), roasted hazelnut / buttery popcorn / clean earthy aroma, small white salt/tyrosine crystals inside muscle fibers.
⚠️ Case Hardening vs. Wet Surface:
• Sticky/slimy exterior: Humidity too high (>84% RH) or blocked fan airflow.
• Thick leathery outer ring with raw wet core: Humidity too low (<70% RH).

📋 Master Butcher's Guide & Backup

1. Post-Slaughter Loading Window:
Load Pork, Lamb & Poultry 1–2 days after slaughter. Load Beef 2–3 days after slaughter. Dry aging hits pH 6.0 (nutty, tender, zero drip loss) vs wet-aged cryovac at pH 5.5 (sour/metallic).
2. Cattle Selection & 10 Butcher Checks:
Choose Heifer (18–22 months) or Ox (24+ months) with strong marbling. Ask for carcasses hung by Tenderstretch / Pelvic suspension as a whole side during rigor mortis, stress-free slaughter, and grain-finished marbling.
3. Drilling & Stocking Rules:
Use a disinfected drill through the vertebral spine bone with a swivel S-hook so zero meat is punctured. Leave 5–8cm airflow gaps. Place salt blocks in a stainless tray underneath to catch drips.
