📖 Master Butcher Checklist

1. Chamber Prep: 1.5°C–2°C | 75–80% RH | UVC ON for Meat/Fish (UVC OFF for Salami/Cheese). Blot meat bone-dry; Fat UP, Bones DOWN.

2. Harvest & Trim: Shave 1–2mm dark pellicle crust with a stiff boning knife. Cut thick 38–50mm steaks.

3. Freezer Traceability: Vacuum seal in barrier bags. Chilled 30–45 days or frozen at -18°C for 12+ months.
