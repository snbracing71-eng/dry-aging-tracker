📖 Tactical Dry Aging Checklist

1. Active Dry Aging (MA400) • Temp: 1.5°C–2°C | RH: 75%–80% | UVC ON.
• Never wash meat with water. Blot bone-dry. Place Fat Side UP, Bones DOWN with 8cm air gaps.
2. Harvesting & Trimming (Pellicle Excision) • Weigh final cut, slice off 1–2mm leathery pellicle crust with a stiff boning knife.
• Portion into thick steaks (38–50mm) so they sear cleanly without overcooking.
3. Storage & Freezer Traceability • Vacuum-seal steaks in barrier bags. Keeps chilled up to 30–45 days or frozen at -18°C for 12+ months with zero purge loss.
