🩺 "Is This Safe?" Troubleshooter

Check what you see or smell inside your cabinet for an instant diagnosis:

⚪ White Chalky Specs / Patchy White Dust
VERDICT: SAFE ✅
Harmless tyrosine/salt crystals forming on the dry pellicle or beneficial white Penicillium mold. Leave alone and trim off normally at harvest.
🟢 Green, Black, or Tall Fuzzy Hair Mold
VERDICT: DANGER ⚠️ (Mycotoxin Risk)
If tiny and isolated early on, wipe immediately with 70% food-grade ethanol or vinegar and drop RH to 75%. If green/black mold penetrates deep into muscle cracks with a putrid smell, discard affected section and sanitize cabinet.
💧 Sticky, Slimy, or Wet Shiny Exterior
VERDICT: HUMIDITY TOO HIGH (>82% RH) ⚠️
Blot meat dry with clean paper towels, ensure 8–10 cm airflow clearance on all sides, empty any liquid around your Himalayan salt block, and lower RH to 74%–76%.
🪨 Rock-Hard Thick Outer Ring (Case Hardening)
VERDICT: HUMIDITY TOO LOW (<70% RH) ⚠️
Outer crust dried too fast and trapped moisture inside the core. Raise cabinet humidity to 78%–80% immediately.
👃 Smell Test: Nutty/Funky vs. Sour/Ammonia
• Buttered popcorn, roasted hazelnut, earthy mushroom, mild blue cheese: 100% Normal & Desired ✅
• Sharp sour vinegar, rotting garbage, or stinging ammonia: Spoilage ⚠️ (Check cabinet temp hasn't risen above 4°C).

📋 Master Dry Aging Checklist

Step 1: Buying at the Butcher (Landig Standard) • Ask for fresh bone-in sub-primals (5–10 days post-slaughter), NOT old cryovac bags sitting in blood purge.
• Keep chine/feather bones ON and the full dorsal fat cap UNTRIMMED.
Step 2: Cleaning & Sanitizing the MA400 • Take the Himalayan salt block out before cleaning.
• Wipe walls and stainless racks with warm water, then spray with 70% Food-Grade Ethanol and pre-chill for 12 hrs.
Step 3: Day 0 Prep & Loading • Wear nitrile gloves. NEVER wash meat with tap water. Blot bone-dry with lint-free paper towels and trim loose hanging flaps.
• Place on rack (Fat Cap UP, Bones DOWN) or hang from bone with 8–10 cm airflow clearance.
Step 4: Harvest, Pellicle & Freezer Vault • Slice off 1–2mm dark pellicle (save for smash burgers or rendering tallow).
• Cut steaks 38–50mm thick. Vacuum-sealed steaks last 30–45 days chilled or 12+ months frozen at -18°C with zero drip loss.
