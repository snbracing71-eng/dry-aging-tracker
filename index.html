📖 AgerPro Checklist

1. Prep: 1.5°C–2°C | 75–80% RH | UVC ON for Meat/Poultry/Fish (OFF for Salami/Cheese). Wipe bone dust clean; hook through bone, never meat.

2. Taste Options: Choose Basic (mild tenderization), Intense (rich nutty/umami flavor), or Intense+ (deepest dry-aged aroma & moisture concentration) when adding a batch.

3. Storage: Vacuum seal after trimming outer crust. Keeps 30–45d chilled or 12+ mos frozen at -18°C.
